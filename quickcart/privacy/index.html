QuickCart+ Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

QuickCart+ is an iPhone app for writing down shopping items quickly, from Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets. This page explains what the app does with your information.

In short
  • There are no accounts, no login and no iCloud sync. Your shopping list is stored on your iPhone, and the developer does not receive it.
  • The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data when ads load, starting after the first-run walkthrough.
  • The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a Google consent form first, and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Deleting the app deletes your list.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of QuickCart+ is Kirade (an individual developer). Questions about privacy: kirade.apps+quickcart@gmail.com.

Person responsible for privacy: Kirade, kirade.apps+quickcart@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

QuickCart+ stores the following only on your iPhone, in storage shared by the app and its widgets:

  • Your shopping list items: the text you typed, when you added each item and when you checked it
  • Whether you have finished the first-run walkthrough

QuickCart+ has no accounts, no server of its own and no cloud sync. The developer does not receive this data. The widgets read the same data on your iPhone to show your items. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), the backup can include this data, as with most apps.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

QuickCart+ shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the main screen. Google's ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK, together with Google's User Messaging Platform SDK for the consent form) starts after you finish the first-run walkthrough, or at launch if you already have. Ads load directly from your iPhone to Google. They do not pass through any server of the developer, and the developer does not receive this data. The app does not give the ad software your shopping list.

According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK collects:

  • Device ID, for advertising and analytics
  • IP address and approximate location (the IP address may be used to estimate the general location of a device)
  • Advertising data, such as ads you have seen
  • Product interactions, such as taps on ads, to improve ad performance
  • Crash logs, performance data and other diagnostic data, to diagnose problems and improve the SDK (performance data may also be used for displaying ads)

Google's consent form software (User Messaging Platform) collects approximate location, performance data and product interactions to run the consent form.

QuickCart+ does not show the iOS tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency). Because it does not ask for that permission, the iOS advertising identifier is not available to the ad software.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Ad choices

  • Users in places where the law requires it, such as the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, see a Google consent form before ads start. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may see ads that are not personalised, or limited ads.
  • To change your choice: in QuickCart+, tap the gear button to open Settings, then Privacy Settings. This item is shown only to users who need it.
  • QuickCart+ does not block any feature based on your ad choices. Ads are part of the main screen and are not an optional feature, so there is no setting that removes them.

4. Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

  • Personalised ads are based on the consent you give in the Google consent form. You can withdraw or change that consent at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • The developer does not receive or keep your personal information. Your list is only on your iPhone, so you can view, change and delete it in the app, or by deleting the app.
  • Google processes ad data under Google's own policies. To use your rights with Google, see Google's Privacy Policy and the page linked in section 3.

5. Deleting data and how long it is kept

  • Delete an item: swipe it in the list. Your other items stay.
  • Clear Done: deletes all items you have marked as purchased. You are asked to confirm, and this cannot be undone.
  • Delete the app: deletes your list and the other data in section 2 from your iPhone.

How long data is kept:

  • Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or delete the app.
  • The developer holds no user data, so there is nothing on the developer's side to delete.
  • Data sent to Google is kept as described in Google's policies (see section 3).

7. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete the data on your iPhone in the app at any time. The developer does not hold your data and does not know who you are, so there is almost nothing to look up. If you still have a request or a complaint, write to kirade.apps+quickcart@gmail.com. For data held by Google, you can also contact Google using the links above.

You can change your ad choices at any time (section 3).

8. TestFlight

If you install a test build through Apple's TestFlight, the developer can see in App Store Connect the email address and name you were invited with, your sessions and crashes (depending on your iOS analytics settings), and any feedback you send, including screenshots. Apple provides this data. QuickCart+ does not collect it itself.

9. Children

QuickCart+ is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has used the app, contact kirade.apps+quickcart@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
