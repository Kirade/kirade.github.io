QuickCart+ Support

Help for the QuickCart+ iPhone app, by Kirade

QuickCart+ is an iPhone app for adding items to your shopping list in about a second, from Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets.

Need help? Write to kirade.apps+quickcart@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what happened.

What QuickCart+ does

  • Add items fast. Tap a widget, type the item and save it.
  • Check items off. Tap an item to mark it as purchased. Swipe it to the right to edit it, or to the left to delete it.
  • Clear Done. Delete all purchased items at once. The app asks you to confirm first.

Add a widget

Lock Screen

Long press the Lock Screen → Customize → Add Widget → search for QuickCart+. Three sizes are available: circular, rectangular and inline.

Home Screen

Long press the Home Screen → tap the + button → search for QuickCart+. Two sizes are available: small and medium. The medium widget also shows your next items to buy.

The same steps appear in the app: open Settings (the gear button) and tap How to Use.

Your data

  • There is no account, no login and no iCloud sync. Your list is stored on your iPhone only, and the developer does not receive it.
  • The list is not shared between your devices. If you use QuickCart+ on more than one iPhone or iPad, each one has its own list.
  • Deleting the app deletes your list. Delete items one by one, or use Clear Done to remove the purchased ones.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Ads and your choices

QuickCart+ shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the main screen.

  • In some regions, such as the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you see a Google consent form. To change your choice, open Settings → Privacy Settings in QuickCart+. It appears only where it is required.
  • QuickCart+ does not block any feature based on your ad choices.

Questions

Where is my list stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no server and no sync between devices.

If I delete the app, is my list gone?

Yes. The list is stored with the app, so deleting the app deletes it.

Where do I change the ad privacy settings?

Open the gear button → Privacy Settings. It is shown only to users who need it.

Which languages does it support?

English and Korean. The app follows your iPhone’s language.
