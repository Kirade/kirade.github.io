Fitted Support

Help for the Fitted iPhone app, by Kirade

Fitted is an iPhone app that builds a resume tailored to a job posting. You paste the posting, an AI interviews you, and the app writes an English resume for that posting.

Need help? Write to kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what happened.

What Fitted does

  • Paste a job posting. Fitted picks out the company, the title and the requirements.
  • Answer an AI interview. The AI asks about your experience. What you say becomes the facts the resume is built from.
  • Check the requirement match. Before the resume is written, Fitted shows which of the posting's requirements your profile already supports. You can answer more questions to fill the gaps.
  • Get an English resume tailored to the posting.
  • Edit it. Each bullet is linked to the facts it is based on, so you can see why it says what it says and change it.
  • Export a PDF. An ad may be shown before the share sheet opens (at most once every 5 minutes). There is no ad for resumes made with a purchase or while a 7-Day Pass is active.

AI and your consent

AI features run only after you tap Allow on the consent screen. The screen says that the AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), is a third-party AI service and lists what is sent. Your name and contact details from your profile are never sent. Anything you type in answers or instructions is sent as written, so please don’t include contact details there.

To turn AI off, turn off Use AI in Settings. Your saved resumes stay on your iPhone, and you can still edit and export them. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Your data

  • There is no account. Your profile, job postings, resumes and interviews are stored on your iPhone.
  • Fitted's server does not store what you send to the AI. It keeps records of your purchases and your free resume, so that your purchases work on all your devices and the free resume is given once per Apple Account. Delete All Data does not remove them, and you can usually get your purchases back with Restore Purchases. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • To remove everything Fitted saved on your iPhone, tap Delete All Data in Settings and confirm. This cannot be undone. Deleting the app also deletes the data.
  • From Fitted 1.1, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog: which features are used and how often, never what you write. To stop, turn off Send Anonymous Usage Statistics in Settings. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it is off unless you turn it on. Delete All Data does not change this switch.

Purchases

Your first resume is free. After that, you can buy more with Apple’s In-App Purchase. Both the 1 More Resume and the 7-Day Pass are one-time purchases. Nothing renews automatically.

  • 1 More Resume: 1 new resume with up to 20 AI rewrites. It never expires, and the resume exports without ads. Your first purchase of it has a lower intro price.
  • 7-Day Pass: 7 days from purchase. You can create up to 3 new resumes every 24 hours, counted from when you buy, so up to 21 in total. Each comes with up to 20 AI rewrites, and there are no ads on any export during the pass.
  • Resumes you’ve already made stay yours to open, edit and export, even after a pass ends.
  • Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so they work on every iPhone where you use Fitted with that Apple Account.

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone, after reinstalling, or after Delete All Data, open Settings in Fitted and, under Purchases, tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. Unused resumes and an active pass come back. The free resume does not come back if it was already used with your Apple Account.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a purchase, an unused resume from it is removed, and so is the time of a refunded pass. Resumes you already made stay on your iPhone.

Finishing a resume

When Fitted starts writing a resume, it sets aside 1 resume for it (your free resume, a purchased resume or one from your pass). If the app closes or the connection drops before you get the resume, Library shows Finish Your Resume. Finish it by the time shown to get your resume without using another one.

If you leave before the resume is made, the resume you set aside usually comes back within a few minutes. Without a connection, or with AI turned off, it can take up to 24 hours. If the resume was already made, it doesn’t come back, and you can finish it from Library instead.

Daily limits

To keep the service available to everyone, AI use has daily limits. Some limits apply to the whole service, so on a busy day you may see the limit message sooner. The day follows Korea time. If you reach a limit, Fitted shows a message such as “You’ve reached today’s limit. Try again after …”. Your saved resumes can still be edited and exported while you wait.

Ads and your choices

Fitted may show an ad from Google AdMob when you export a PDF. There is no ad for resumes made with a purchase or while a 7-Day Pass is active.

  • In some regions, such as the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you may see a consent form. To change your choice, open Settings → Privacy Choices in Fitted. It appears only where it is required.
  • To allow or refuse tracking, open the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Fitted does not block any feature based on your ad choices.

Feedback

Fitted sometimes asks one short question that you answer with a tap, such as how making a resume went. You can skip it. To vote for the next feature or write a comment, open Settings → Send Feedback (shown while AI is on). Feedback is kept without your install ID or purchase IDs, so we can’t reply to it. The developer may use Anthropic’s Claude to summarise it. Please don’t include personal information. If you need an answer, write to kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com.

Questions

Is Fitted free?

Downloading Fitted and making your first resume are free. An ad may be shown when you export that resume as a PDF. More resumes are in-app purchases (see Purchases above). Editing and exporting resumes you’ve already made stay free.

Why does the 7-Day Pass allow only 3 resumes every 24 hours?

Each resume uses a lot of AI work, so the pass spreads it out. The limit is a pace, not a cap on the pass: over 7 days you can make up to 21 resumes (within the daily limits below). The purchase screen shows when you can make the next one.

Which languages does it support?

The resumes Fitted writes are in English. The app itself is available in English and Korean.

Does it work offline?

Your library, editing and PDF export work offline. The AI features need an internet connection.

Fitted says it couldn’t verify this copy of the app.

Update Fitted from the App Store and try again. If it keeps happening, write to us and include the app version shown in Settings.
