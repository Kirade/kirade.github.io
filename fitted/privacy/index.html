Fitted Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

Fitted is an iPhone app. You paste a job posting, and an AI interviews you and writes a resume tailored to that posting. This page explains what the app does with your information.

In short
  • There are no accounts. Your profile, job postings, resumes and interviews are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you agree to the AI feature, the text needed for it is sent through Fitted's server to Anthropic (Claude). Your name and contact details are not sent.
  • Fitted's server does not store what you send. It keeps technical logs for about 14 days.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob when you export a PDF. Google's ad software collects some device data, starting when you open the resume editor or Settings.
  • You can turn AI off or delete all data in Settings at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of Fitted is Kirade (an individual developer). Questions about privacy: kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com.

Person responsible for privacy: Kirade, kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Fitted stores the following only on your iPhone (in the app's own storage):

  • Your profile: name, email, phone, location, links, work experience, facts you told the app, education, skills and certifications
  • Job postings you paste
  • Resumes the app creates
  • Interview conversations

Fitted has no accounts and no cloud sync. A PDF you export goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), the backup can include this data, as with most apps.

3. AI features

AI features run only after you agree on the consent screen. The consent screen names Anthropic as the AI provider. You can turn AI off in Settings.

What is sent

When you use an AI feature, Fitted sends the following:

  • Your interview answers and the AI's questions in that conversation
  • Your career facts, work history (company, title, dates and locations of jobs), education, skills and certifications
  • Job postings you paste, and the company name, title and requirements taken from them
  • When you ask the AI to rewrite a resume line: that line, the facts it is based on, and any instruction you type

Your name and contact details (email, phone, the location and links in your contact info) are never sent. Please do not write contact details in your answers or instructions, because anything you type is sent as written.

Where it goes

Your iPhone → Fitted's server (AWS Lambda, Seoul region, ap-northeast-2) → Anthropic API.

  • Fitted's server builds the request and passes it on. It does not store the content of requests or responses.
  • Anthropic PBC (United States) processes the content to provide the AI features and returns the result. Fitted asks Anthropic to process requests in any available region, so processing can also take place outside the United States.

What Anthropic does with it

Fitted uses Anthropic's API. According to Anthropic's published policies (checked on the pages listed below), for API customers:

  • Anthropic automatically deletes inputs and outputs from its backend within 30 days of receiving or generating them. It may keep them longer when needed to enforce its Usage Policy (up to 2 years if flagged as a violation) or when the law requires.
  • Anthropic does not train its models on inputs or outputs from the API (Commercial Terms, Section B). Data is processed under Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum (Section C).

Anthropic's policies can change. Please read the current ones: how long Anthropic stores data, Commercial Terms, Anthropic's own Privacy Policy.

4. Install ID and server records

When the app first starts, it creates a random ID (a UUID) for your installation. It is not tied to your name or your Apple ID. The app sends it with each AI request so the server can apply a daily usage limit. The server turns it into a hash and uses only the hash. A hash is not anonymous: it can still link requests from the same installation.

The server keeps two kinds of records:

  • Technical logs (Amazon CloudWatch, kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically): which endpoint was called, result status, processing time, the model used, token counts, estimated cost, counts of resume lines removed or flagged by checks, and the hashed install ID. Logs never contain your answers, job postings, resume text or contact details. Fitted's own logs do not include your IP address.
  • Daily limit counters (Amazon DynamoDB): the number of AI requests per hashed install ID, and the total daily spend of the service. Each item expires two days after the end of that day (Korea time) and is then removed automatically. Deletion is not instant.

Deleting the app, or using Delete All Data, discards the install ID. The app creates a new one the next time it needs it.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Fitted shows an interstitial ad from Google AdMob before the share sheet when you export a PDF. Google's ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) starts when you open the resume editor or Settings, and it loads ads in advance. Ads load directly from your iPhone to Google. They do not pass through Fitted's server, and Fitted does not receive this data.

According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK collects:

  • IP address (may be used to estimate the general location of a device)
  • Crash logs, to diagnose problems and improve the SDK
  • Performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate or energy usage (may also be used for displaying ads, including sharing with other entities that display ads)
  • Device ID, such as the advertising identifier, for advertising and analytics
  • Advertising data, such as ads you have seen
  • Other product interactions, such as taps and video views, to improve ad performance

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ad choices

  • Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may see a Google consent form. On iOS, if you have not yet made a tracking choice, you may see a short explanation and then the iOS tracking permission prompt. If you do not allow tracking or personalised ads, you may see ads that are not personalised, limited ads, or no ads.
  • To change your choice: in Fitted, Settings → Privacy Choices (shown only to users who need it); on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Fitted does not block any feature based on your ad choices. Delete All Data does not change them.

6. Turning AI off and deleting data

  • Turn AI off (Settings → Use AI): Fitted stops sending anything to the AI immediately and cancels requests that are in progress. Content that was already sent cannot be taken back. It stays with Anthropic under its retention policy (see section 3), and a request already in progress may already be with Anthropic. Your saved data stays on your iPhone.
  • Delete All Data (Settings): deletes the profile, job postings, resumes, interviews, exported PDFs, AI consent and install ID from this iPhone. This cannot be undone. Fitted's server does not hold your content, and its logs are deleted after about 14 days.
  • Delete the app: this also deletes the data on your iPhone.

How long data is kept, and how it is deleted:

  • Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it, use Delete All Data, or delete the app.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • Daily limit records expire as described in section 4 and are then deleted automatically.
  • Data sent to Anthropic or Google is kept as described in sections 3, 5 and 7.

8. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete the data on your iPhone in the app at any time. Fitted's server does not keep your content and does not know who you are, so it has almost nothing to look up. If you still have a request or a complaint, write to kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com. For data held by Anthropic or Google, you can also contact them using the links above.

You can withdraw your consent to AI at any time (section 6). You can change ad choices at any time (section 5).

9. TestFlight

If you install a test build through Apple's TestFlight, the developer can see in App Store Connect the email address and name you were invited with, your sessions and crashes (depending on your iOS analytics settings), and any feedback you send, including screenshots. Apple provides this data. Fitted does not collect it itself.

10. Children

Fitted is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has used the app, contact kirade.apps+fitted@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. If a change affects what is sent to the AI, the app asks for your consent again.
