DailyScrum Support

Help for the DailyScrum iPhone app, by Kirade

DailyScrum: Stand-up Meeting is an iPhone app for preparing your daily stand-up in about two minutes. Each day has a note with three sections, Yesterday, Today and Blockers, and one tap copies the whole note as text you can paste into Slack or anywhere else. View on the App Store.

Need help? Write to kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use and what happened. Do not include the contents of your notes.

What DailyScrum does

  • One note per day. The app opens on today’s note. Write what you did yesterday, what you will do today and what blocks you.
  • Copy All. Copies the note as text, ready to paste into your team chat.
  • Other days. Go back to the list to open an earlier or a later day. You can delete a note you no longer need.

Send feedback

Open the list, tap the gear button to open Settings, then tap Send Feedback. Your mail app opens with a message to the address above. Before you send it, the message contains the app name and version, your iOS version and your iPhone model, so that the developer can reproduce problems. It never contains your notes. You can edit or delete anything in the message before you send it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Your data

  • There is no account, no login and no server of the developer. Your notes are stored on your iPhone only, and the developer does not receive them.
  • The text of your notes is not sent anywhere, and it is not part of the anonymous usage statistics or of feedback mail.
  • Deleting the app deletes your notes. Deleting a note removes just that one.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Ads and your choices

DailyScrum shows a banner ad from Google AdMob.

  • In some regions, such as the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you see a Google consent form. To change your choice, open Settings → Privacy Settings in DailyScrum. It appears only where it is required.
  • Starting with version 1.2, you see a short explanation and then the iOS tracking permission prompt, once. To allow or refuse tracking later, open the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • DailyScrum does not block any feature based on your ad choices.

Anonymous usage statistics

Starting with version 1.2, DailyScrum sends anonymous usage statistics so the developer can see which features are used. The text of your notes is never included. To turn this off or on, open Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it is off until you turn it on. Details are in section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no server and no sync between devices.

If I delete the app, are my notes gone?

Yes. The notes are stored with the app, so deleting the app deletes them.

Where do I change the ad privacy settings?

Open the gear button → Privacy Settings. It is shown only to users who need it. To allow or refuse tracking, open the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I stop the usage statistics?

Open the gear button → turn off Share anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent after that, and the app works the same.
