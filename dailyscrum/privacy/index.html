DailyScrum Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

DailyScrum: Stand-up Meeting is an iPhone and iPad app for preparing your daily stand-up with Yesterday, Today and Blockers notes. This page explains what the app does with your information.

In short
  • There are no accounts and no login. Your notes (Yesterday, Today and Blockers) are stored on your device only. They are never sent to a server, and they are not part of usage statistics or feedback mail.
  • The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data when ads load. The iOS advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a Google consent form first, and can change their choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Starting with version 1.2, DailyScrum sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog to learn how features are used. It never sends your notes or anything you type, and you can turn this off in Settings. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it is off until you turn it on.
  • Deleting the app deletes your notes.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of DailyScrum is Kirade (an individual developer). Questions about privacy: kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com.

Person responsible for privacy: Kirade, kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

DailyScrum stores the following only on your device:

  • Your notes: the text you typed in the Yesterday, Today and Blockers sections, and the date of each note
  • Your app settings, such as whether anonymous usage statistics are on

DailyScrum has no accounts and no server of its own for your notes. The developer does not receive this data, and the app does not give your notes to the ad software or to the usage statistics service. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your device (to iCloud or a computer), the backup can include this data, as with most apps.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

DailyScrum shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google's ad software (Google Mobile Ads SDK, together with Google's User Messaging Platform SDK for the consent form) starts when the app starts. Ads load directly from your device to Google. They do not pass through any server of the developer, and the developer does not receive this data. The app does not give the ad software your notes.

According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK collects:

  • Device ID, for advertising and analytics
  • IP address and approximate location (the IP address may be used to estimate the general location of a device)
  • Advertising data, such as ads you have seen
  • Product interactions, such as taps on ads, to improve ad performance
  • Crash logs, performance data and other diagnostic data, to diagnose problems and improve the SDK (performance data may also be used for displaying ads)

Google's consent form software (User Messaging Platform) collects approximate location, performance data and product interactions to run the consent form.

Tracking permission (version 1.2 and later). When the app starts, Google's User Messaging Platform shows users in all regions a short explanation and then the iOS tracking permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency), once. If you allow tracking, Google's ad software can use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalised ads. If you do not allow it, the ad software cannot use it. DailyScrum does not block any feature based on your choice, and you can change it in the Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Ad choices

  • Users in places where the law requires it, such as the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, see a Google consent form before ads start. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may see ads that are not personalised, or limited ads.
  • Users in all regions see a short explanation and then the iOS tracking permission prompt, once (see above). If you do not allow tracking, you may see ads that are not personalised.
  • To change your choice: in DailyScrum, open Settings, then Privacy Settings (shown only to users who need it); on your device, open the Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • DailyScrum does not block any feature based on your ad choices. Ads are part of the app and are not an optional feature, so there is no setting that removes them.

4. Usage statistics (PostHog, version 1.2 and later)

To understand how features are used and to improve the app, DailyScrum sends usage events to the analytics service of PostHog, Inc.

What is sent

  • What happened in the app and its kind: the app was opened (with a range for how many notes you have: 0, 1–5, 6–20 or 21 or more); a note was opened (where it was opened from, and whether the day is today, in the past or in the future); a section was edited (which of Yesterday, Today or Blockers, not what you wrote); Copy All was used (how many of the three sections were filled in); a note was deleted; Settings was opened; Send Feedback was tapped
  • App name and version, build type (App Store, TestFlight or development), iOS version, device model and type, language, time zone, screen size, network type (Wi-Fi or cellular)
  • A random identifier created when the app is installed. It is deleted with the app and replaced when you reinstall.

What is not sent

  • The text of your notes or anything you type, the number of characters, the dates of your notes, your name, your email address, the advertising identifier (IDFA), device identifiers such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV), or location
  • The app does not create a user profile and does not link usage events to anything that identifies you. PostHog receives your IP address while the data is transmitted, but the project is set not to derive location from it and not to store it.

Where it is stored. PostHog stores the data in the United States.

How to turn it off or on. Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent after you turn it off, and the app works the same. For users whose App Store country is in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the switch is off by default and nothing is sent until you turn it on. The app does not show a pop-up asking about this. Users elsewhere have it on by default.

Retention. Events are kept by PostHog for 1 year and are no longer queryable after that. They are used only to improve the app.

5. Feedback mail (version 1.2 and later)

Settings → Send Feedback opens your mail app with a message to kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com. The message contains the app name and version, your iOS version and your device model, and never your notes. You can edit it before sending. The developer receives it only if you press Send, and uses it only to answer you and to fix problems. The mail passes through your own mail provider.

6. Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

  • Personalised ads are based on the consent you give in the Google consent form. You can withdraw or change that consent at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are off by default for you. They are sent only if you turn on Share anonymous usage statistics in Settings, and you can turn them off again at any time.
  • The developer does not receive or keep your notes. They are only on your device, so you can view, change and delete them in the app, or by deleting the app.
  • Google and PostHog process data under their own policies. To use your rights with them, see Google's Privacy Policy, PostHog's Privacy Policy and the pages linked in sections 3 and 4.

7. Deleting data and how long it is kept

  • Delete a note: in the app. Your other notes stay.
  • Delete the app: deletes your notes and the other data in section 2 from your device. It also deletes the random identifier used for usage statistics.
  • Stop usage statistics: turn off Share anonymous usage statistics in Settings.

How long data is kept:

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or delete the app.
  • The developer holds none of your notes, so there is nothing of them on the developer's side to delete.
  • Usage events already sent are kept by PostHog for 1 year. Because they are tied only to a random identifier, the developer cannot tell which events are yours and cannot delete them one by one.
  • Data sent to Google is kept as described in Google's policies (see section 3).

9. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete the data on your device in the app at any time. The developer does not hold your notes and does not know who you are, so there is almost nothing to look up. If you still have a request or a complaint, write to kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com. For data held by Google or PostHog, you can also contact them using the links above.

You can change your ad choices and your usage statistics choice at any time (sections 3 and 4).

10. TestFlight

If you install a test build through Apple's TestFlight, the developer can see in App Store Connect the email address and name you were invited with, your sessions and crashes (depending on your iOS analytics settings), and any feedback you send, including screenshots. Apple provides this data. DailyScrum does not collect it itself.

11. Children

DailyScrum is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has used the app, contact kirade.apps+dailyscrum@gmail.com.
